Who We Are

A digital studio powered by
passion & AI tools.

Peqa Technology (PeqaTech) is a Malaysia-registered digital studio specialising in web design, development, and digital strategy. We leverage modern tools, AI, and proven templates to deliver polished, professional websites — efficiently and affordably.

Founded in 2018 and registered with Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia (SSM) under PG0510758-T, we have delivered over 50 projects — from one-page portfolios to full corporate websites and conference platforms. We work with founders, brands, and businesses who want results without the agency price tag.
